# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in improving the examples, documentation, or open-source work in this profile repository.

## Before contributing

- Open an issue to discuss substantial changes.
- Keep contributions relevant to the portfolio and its documentation.
- Do not add private, confidential, or personally identifying information.
- Never include credentials, access tokens, private keys, or production data.

## Pull requests

Please provide:

1. A clear summary of the change.
2. The reason the change is useful.
3. Relevant screenshots or examples for visual changes.
4. Testing or verification performed, where applicable.

## Style

- Prefer clear, direct documentation.
- Use descriptive file and folder names.
- Keep technical claims accurate and verifiable.
- Distinguish ideas, prototypes, active development, and live products.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the repository's license.
