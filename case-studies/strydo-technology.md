# Strydo Technology — Digital Product and Web Services

**Status:** Technology business and service portfolio  
**Role represented here:** Business direction, service definition, and web-product planning  
**Implementation claim:** This case study summarizes the service areas explored; it does not claim every listed service has a public production deployment.

## Business direction

Strydo Technology is a technology-services initiative focused on helping businesses and founders establish and improve their digital presence.

## Service areas explored

- Website design and development.
- Business and administration dashboards.
- Booking, real-estate, lending, and marketplace website solutions.
- Fintech and payment-interface development.
- Search visibility, digital promotion, and website monetization support.
- Technical troubleshooting and deployment assistance.

## Delivery approach

A disciplined engagement should begin with discovery and scope definition, followed by a proposal, agreed milestones, implementation, testing, handover documentation, and post-launch support where agreed.

## Engineering priorities

- Responsive and accessible interfaces.
- Maintainable application structure.
- Secure authentication and authorization.
- Input validation and safe database access.
- Clear integration boundaries for third-party services.
- Backups, deployment notes, and operational handover.

## What this work demonstrates

The ability to think across both the technical and business sides of digital delivery: understanding client needs, translating them into scope, and planning the application and operational requirements.
